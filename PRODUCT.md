# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers, editors, and other users browsing the STAVNET bibliographic database to inspect people, books, organizations, and the links between them.

## Product Purpose

Provide a compact, reliable, multilingual reference interface for bibliographic and biographical records, with fast cross-navigation and faithful data presentation.

## Brand Personality

Exact, archival, trustworthy, dense.

## Anti-references

Marketing-style landing pages, decorative dashboard chrome, inconsistent controls, and unclear navigation patterns.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the FileMaker reference geometry and information hierarchy.
- Keep the interface dense but readable.
- Make linked records easy to inspect and resolve.
- Favor predictable data-table behavior over decorative UI.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support the six project languages, including proper RTL handling for Hebrew and Arabic. Keep keyboard navigation clear, maintain strong contrast, and avoid motion that interferes with reading.
